# **App Name**: AgendaPro

## Core Features:

- AI WhatsApp Scheduler: An intelligent tool for customers to book and manage appointments conversationally through WhatsApp, supported by AI to handle common scenarios.
- Bilingual AI Communication: A generative AI tool that understands and responds in both Spanish and English, adapting to the user's language preference during the conversation.
- Business Management Dashboard: A web interface allowing service providers to configure their availability, services, FAQs, and view all scheduled appointments.
- Automated Client Reminders: System to automatically send appointment reminders to clients via WhatsApp at predefined intervals before their booking.
- FAQ & Business Information Editor: A tool for businesses to easily create and update answers to frequently asked questions and manage core business details the AI uses.
- Real-time Admin Handoff: Provides businesses with the ability to take over WhatsApp conversations from the AI bot for complex inquiries and receives critical notifications.
- Early Access Lead Capture: A secure form to collect information from businesses interested in early access to the platform for lead management.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep forest green (#1A5C2E). This professional hue evokes growth, reliability, and connects with natural elements, aligning with the trustworthy and efficient business automation.
- Background color: A very light, desaturated greenish-white (#F4F8F4). This subtle tint provides a clean, unobtrusive canvas, ensuring the content remains the focal point while maintaining visual harmony with the primary color.
- Accent color: A vibrant greenish-yellow (#DBF993). This bright and energetic hue offers strong contrast against the primary, highlighting calls to action and important information with a fresh, lively appeal.
- Headline font: 'Syne' (sans-serif) for its impactful and modern presence, suitable for strong headings and branding. Body font: 'Instrument Sans' (sans-serif) for readability and a contemporary feel in longer texts. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Utilize simple, context-appropriate emoji (e.g., 📅, 🌐, 🤖) for clear visual representation of features and steps, maintaining a friendly yet functional aesthetic.
- A modern, grid-based layout for content organization that ensures responsiveness across devices, collapsing multi-column sections to a single column on smaller screens, and featuring a sticky header for consistent navigation.
- Subtle and swift transitions (e.g., color changes, slight scaling, soft box shadows) on interactive elements like buttons and cards, providing a polished and engaging user experience without being distracting.
